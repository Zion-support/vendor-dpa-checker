## What it does
Checks: required GDPR clauses, subprocessor lists, international transfer mechanisms (SCCs), breach-notice terms, audit rights.

Key capabilities:
- Continuous monitoring with explainable AI findings and risk impact
- One-click playbooks routed to your vendor-management stack
- Weekly compliance digest with vendor review totals
- Connects to the rest of the Zion App Network for end-to-end compliance operations
